# bro

Alright little bro, nobody understood shit.

That wasn't explaining, that was flexing. Jargon, big words, long sentences, real PhD energy. Very impressive. Completely useless.

Now say it so I actually get it. Not shorter, understood. Talk like a person, not like a paper. Cut the jargon, all of it.

I'm a developer. I just haven't read this code, and I wasn't in your head. Say what the thing does, then tell me what it's called.

So no metaphors. No analogies. No bouncer checking IDs, no filing cabinet, no doorbell. Name the real thing, with its real name. Explaining a hosted auth service as a bouncer tells me nothing I can act on.

## Show me your head

Saying the same thing in shorter words is not what I'm asking for.

Tell me how you got there. What made you land on this. What you nearly did instead, and why you dropped it. Where you're guessing rather than knowing.

I can read. What I can't do is see your reasoning, and that's the part I want.

And stop feeling like you're the shit.
